# Code of Conduct

DLL Mass Units follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and keep discussion about the work. Harassment,
personal attacks and discriminatory language are not accepted in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other
project space.

**Reporting.** Contact the maintainers privately through [digilogiclabs.com](https://digilogiclabs.com). Reports are handled
confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, or block participants who break this code.
